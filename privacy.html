Privacy Policy

REEL COMMAND - last updated September 12, 2026

REEL COMMAND is a personal, single-operator tool. It is not offered to the public and has exactly one user: Roderick Howe, who owns and operates the two YouTube channels it manages (GarageReel and WornTapes). This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how it is handled.

What data is accessed

Using Google OAuth and the owner's own Google account consent, REEL COMMAND accesses only the following, and only for the owner's own two YouTube channels:

  • Uploading videos and setting their titles, descriptions, and playlists (YouTube Data API).
  • Reading channel statistics such as subscriber counts, views, and traffic sources (YouTube Analytics API).

Where data is stored

All data stays on the owner's own computer. OAuth tokens are stored locally as files on that machine and are never uploaded, synced to a third-party server, or shared with anyone. No data collected through these APIs is sold, rented, or provided to any third party for advertising or any other purpose.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties. The tool talks directly to Google's own APIs and to no other external service with this data.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored authorization tokens can be deleted at any time by removing them from the owner's computer, and access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
