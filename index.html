REEL COMMAND

A personal YouTube automation pipeline

REEL COMMAND is a privately-run, personal-use tool built and operated by Roderick Howe to generate and schedule short-form videos for two YouTube channels he owns and operates: GarageReel and WornTapes.

It uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, under Roderick's own Google account authorization, to upload videos, manage playlists, and read channel performance stats for those two channels only. It is not a public service, is not distributed to other users, and does not access any account other than the owner's own YouTube channels.

See the privacy policy for details on data use.
